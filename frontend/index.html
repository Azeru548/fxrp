01How it works

One open contract, three calls. The full loop runs on-chain: create, pay, withdraw.

01

Create

Merchant prices an invoice in USD or fixed FXRP and mints a payment link in one call.

02

Pay

Any wallet opens the link, approves the token, pays. USD invoices fetch the FTSOv2 XRP/USD price on-chain.

03

Withdraw

Settled FXRP sits in the contract until the merchant pulls it. Full custody, no custodian.

02Why Flare

Rowan only works because Flare settles the whole loop natively.

FAssets · FXRP

Invoices settle in FXRP, the FAsset that makes XRP spendable in EVM DeFi: the interoperable asset Bounty 1 is about.

FTSOv2 ORACLE

getFeedByIdInWei(XRP/USD) converts USD amounts at payment time; the fee is paid in FLR and excess refunded atomically.

Portable NETWORKS

One constructor arg (the FTSOv2 address). The same contract deploys to Coston2, Songbird, and Flare Mainnet unchanged.

04For developers

Any EVM app can accept FXRP payments: one public contract, three calls, no server to run.

// merchant
createInvoice(payee, token, pricing, amount, memo)
// any wallet
pay(id)
// merchant pulls settled FXRP
withdraw(id)

Payment link format: #/pay?c=<FxrpPay address>&id=<invoice id>. Drop it in any checkout, email, or QR code. The payer lands with the invoice pre-loaded.
